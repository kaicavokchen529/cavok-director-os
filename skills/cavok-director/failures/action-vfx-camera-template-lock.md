# Failure: Action–VFX–Camera Template Lock

## Symptom

Every similar action receives the same visual package: kicks always use a low angle and speed trail, heavy hits always use slow motion plus shake and shockwave, ability charges always orbit, and finishers always use maximum particles and close impact coverage.

## Diagnosis

The plan mapped an action label directly to VFX, camera movement, and shot size without resolving action phase, force vector, audience information, contact class, environment, sequence history, or after-state.

## Fix

1. Restore the legal action and before-state.
2. Identify the phase and physical/graphic vector shown in this shot.
3. Choose what the audience must read.
4. Assign VFX by function, including the option of no added VFX.
5. Select shot function, camera relation, and the smallest scale that preserves every required cause.
6. Compare clarity-led, force/performance-led, and expressive bundles.
7. Show contact, consequence, residual, and the changed after-state.
8. Check recent bundles and reject unmotivated repetition.

## Preventive rule

Never approve “action type → fixed visual package.” Approve a bundle only after it passes source, mechanism, vector, visibility, contact, intensity, environment, continuity, medium, and execution gates.
