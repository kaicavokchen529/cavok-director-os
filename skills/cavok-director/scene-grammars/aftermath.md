# Aftermath Grammar

Impact tail → temporary sensory imbalance → body recovery → environmental evidence → damage/information read → relationship/power read → decision or silence → exit state.

Do not end at the explosion. Let breath, balance, cloth, smoke, steam, debris, ringing sound, altered light and damaged space prove what happened. Often reduce camera motion and allow duration.

Use stable wide/medium or slow withdrawal when consequence is the subject.

Failure: immediate reset, VFX residue vanishes, characters emotionally unaffected, camera rushes into next beat before audience reads cost.
