# Character Introduction Grammar

Purpose: establish identity, status and behavioral essence without turning the entrance into a poster pose.

Entry → environmental or social evidence → partial/functional reveal → reaction or relationship context → full readable presence → behavior that proves identity → exit into story action.

Rules:
- Let the environment or another character give the entrance meaning.
- Full-body reveal is earned when costume, posture, weapon or relationship matters.
- Preserve natural weight, gaze and task; do not freeze for admiration.
- Camera may settle and hold after discovery rather than continuously orbit.
- Sound can precede image when mystery helps.

Failure: instant beauty-shot reveal with no story information, repeated hero angles, identity shown before audience has a question.
