# Boss Reveal Grammar

Entry normality → anomaly/evidence → protagonist notices → delayed partial clue → POV confirmation → scale/power reveal → reaction → controlled demonstration → new threat state.

Use sound, shadow, environmental response, subordinates, damage or silence before full exposure. Establish a human/environment scale reference before monumental size. The reveal should change tactical or emotional state.

Camera duties: protect discovery order; use negative space, foreground obstruction, tilt/reveal, controlled push or static scale. Hold long enough to read consequence.

Failure: showing the boss clearly in shot one, drone-only scale, excessive orbit, demonstration with no effect on environment or characters.
