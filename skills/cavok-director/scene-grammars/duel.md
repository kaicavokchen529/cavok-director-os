# Duel Grammar

Measure/read opponent → first probe → answer → tactical adjustment → advantage shift → damage/cost → escalation → decisive commitment → result → reset/aftermath.

Every exchange must change advantage, distance, resources, damage, knowledge or decision. Establish attack axis and preferred range. Protect setup, contact/near miss, momentum transfer and recovery.

Camera: spatial master plus selective emphasis; stable lateral/full-body coverage when technique matters. Close detail only for decisive information.

Failure: random move collection, both fighters attacking without tactical response, every hit equally emphasized.
