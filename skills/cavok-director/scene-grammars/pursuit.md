# Pursuit Grammar

Route anchor → pursuit objective → obstacle → adaptation → distance change → route change → near-capture/reversal → decisive escape/capture → aftermath.

Track geography, screen direction, relative distance and route landmarks. Re-anchor after major turns, vertical changes, vehicles or teleports. Speed comes from obstacle density, changing distance, focal compression, sound and selective cuts—not random camera shake.

Show cause before consequence: foot plant, turn, collision, stumble, recovery.

Failure: endless running, no distance change, geography reset every shot, camera faster than the characters without purpose.
