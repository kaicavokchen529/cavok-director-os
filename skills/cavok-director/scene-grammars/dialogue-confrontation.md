# Dialogue Confrontation Grammar

Entry relationship → stated tactic → resistance → subtext leak → information/power shift → tactic change → interruption or decision → new relationship state.

Blocking carries power: who approaches, yields, sits, stands, controls exits, shares frame, breaks eye contact or invades space. Do not cut to close-up merely because a line is important; move closer only when access or pressure changes.

Sound: pauses must represent thinking, listening, suppression or tactic change. Overlap and interruption are power tools.

Failure: shot/reverse-shot autopilot, identical performance intensity, random push-ins, no visible power change.
