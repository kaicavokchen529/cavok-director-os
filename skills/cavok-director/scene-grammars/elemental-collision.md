# Elemental Collision Grammar

Establish both sources → show propagation paths → anticipation as systems approach → first contact → mutual material change → compression/deflection → peak interaction → secondary media/debris → environment response → dissipation/residue.

The collision is not a generic explosion. Both systems must alter each other according to material, temperature, momentum and confinement. Preserve contact location and force direction.

Example ice/fire: flame thins/diverts; ice surface melts; water flashes to steam; thermal cracks spread; fragments inherit momentum; steam and leaves move later; wet/cooling residue remains.

Failure: spherical flash replaces physics, equal effects from unequal forces, all debris starts same frame.
