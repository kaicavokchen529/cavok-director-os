# Test 01 — Two-Person Dialogue

Brief: two former allies meet in a quiet room. A knows B betrayed the team; B believes the betrayal is still secret. No violence.

Must test: information asymmetry, subtext, relationship blocking, restrained camera, dialogue pauses/interruptions, motivated move or stillness.

Fail if: automatic shot/reverse-shot, constant push-in, betrayal revealed visually before A chooses to expose it, generic 'angry' acting.
