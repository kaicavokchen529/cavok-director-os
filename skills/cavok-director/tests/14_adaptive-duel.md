# Test 14 — Adaptive Duel

## Brief

Design a short duel between two capable fighters. Fighter A initially succeeds with one tactical pattern. Fighter B must observe evidence, adapt without mind-reading, and force Fighter A to change objective, range, timing, route, or tool. Do not use named legacy characters, a fixed martial-arts combination, or a predetermined finishing move.

## Requirements

- Define capability boundaries without locking a sequence.
- Maintain legal distance, balance, support, limb, weapon, injury, momentum, and control states.
- Give both fighters an objective and credible agency.
- Make the adaptation visibly evidence-driven.
- Give every retained action a tactical, spatial, physical, informational, or dramatic state change.
- Show why initiative transfers.
- Preserve an observable before/action/after state across cuts.
- Let camera or graphic treatment clarify the exchange rather than create missing causality.

## Critical Failures

- Opponent waits passively for attacks or repeats a failed response without cause.
- A fighter performs an action that is illegal from the preceding state.
- Capability binding becomes a mandatory combo or guaranteed ending.
- Initiative changes without observable cause.
- Decorative actions survive only to fill duration or spectacle.
- A cut invisibly resets stance, hands, weapon, injury, restraint, or momentum.
