# Test 19 — Action–VFX–Camera Orchestration

## Brief

Design a short mixed physical/supernatural confrontation in a damaged industrial hall. One fighter evades a launched projectile, uses a grounded grapple and throw, then faces an opponent charging and releasing a directional pressure field. The sequence must make each action readable while varying VFX, camera relation, shot function, and scale by causal need rather than by a fixed style package.

## Requirements

- Confirm every retained action is legal from body, distance, support, weapon/ability, damage, momentum, control, and environment state.
- Build an orchestration packet for the projectile evasion, grapple/throw, and pressure-field release.
- For each beat, identify action phase/vector, contact class, required audience information, VFX function, shot function, camera relation, shot size, temporal treatment, consequence, residual, and after-state.
- Generate clarity-led, force/performance-led, and expressive bundles internally for at least one major beat.
- Allow minimal or no added VFX for the grapple when body mechanics, friction, cloth tension, breath, sound, and contact carry the event.
- Keep grips, leverage, support feet, pivot, release and landing readable during the throw.
- Establish projectile source, route, clearance, environmental wake and arrival state without using blur or trail as a substitute for displacement.
- Show the field's source, boundary, propagation, body/environment response, pressure direction and dissipation.
- Track recent action–VFX–camera combinations and avoid automatic orbit, shake, slow motion, close impact and maximum particle density.

## Critical failures

- An action label directly determines one fixed VFX/camera/scale package.
- Particles, glow, blur, shake, or temporal effects hide action mechanics or contact.
- The grapple is treated like an energy attack without an approved ability.
- Kick/throw/fall mechanics are cropped so support and landing cannot be read.
- Projectile or field direction contradicts camera geography or continuity.
- Contact produces spectacle without force transfer, consequence, residual, or changed after-state.
- Expressive camera treatment replaces tactical legality or opponent agency.
