# 11 Spatial Previs

## Brief

A three-level industrial interior contains a ground-floor loading bay, a mezzanine catwalk, one metal stair, two support columns, a rolling door, and a fixed sodium practical above the rolling door. Character A begins behind the left support column on the ground floor. Character B begins on the mezzanine near the stair landing. A must cross the bay, use the stair, and intercept B before B reaches the opposite catwalk exit.

The sequence requires one forward angle, one reverse angle, one low-angle action shot, and one camera move that travels with A up the stair.

## Requirements

Design the scene using the CAVOK production/previs gates before detailed coverage.

Show:

- Fact / Observation / Inference / Director Proposal / Unknown separation;
- readiness level;
- one consistent world-space map described in text;
- character routes and elevations;
- primary action axis and any motivated crossing;
- camera positions that can physically see the claimed backgrounds;
- the fixed sodium light anchor from all coverage;
- reverse angle derived from the same world, not a mirrored room;
- lens choices only after geometry is resolved.

## Critical Failures

- forward and reverse views imply incompatible room layouts;
- the stair, columns, rolling door, or light anchor change world position without cause;
- camera or performer path travels through solid structure;
- focal length is used to explain a geometry contradiction;
- a camera move is proposed before the performer route can support it.
