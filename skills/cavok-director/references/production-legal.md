# Production, Rights, and Release

## Convert shots into work

For each shot record owner, status, priority, duration, dependencies, assets, model or engine, generation/render budget, review gate, acceptance criteria, and fallback.

```text
Sequence / shot:
Creative objective:
Deliverable and technical spec:
Dependencies:
Responsible role:
Estimated attempts / render cost / schedule:
Risk level:
Acceptance criteria:
Fallback or simplification:
Approval status:
```

## Plan dependencies

Do not begin expensive final generation before identity, layout, action, camera, and effect design are approved. Use gates:

1. story and rights;
2. visual development and continuity;
3. previs and technical feasibility;
4. shot production;
5. editorial and VFX review;
6. sound, color, and mastering;
7. legal, safety, accessibility, and release review.

Track revision cause separately: creative change, continuity error, model failure, technical defect, missing asset, rights issue, or stakeholder request.

## Rights and provenance

Before publication, confirm authorization for characters, scripts, likenesses, voices, music, footage, images, logos, fonts, models, textures, plugins, and datasets where applicable. Preserve licenses, releases, source links, invoices, and generation records.

Do not assume public availability means commercial permission. Flag recognizable people, copyrighted characters, brand marks, cloned voices, unlicensed music, and confidential material for qualified review.

## Safety and content review

Review violence, weapons, minors, sexual content, self-harm, hate, dangerous imitation, medical claims, flashing imagery, and platform restrictions. For physical production, require qualified safety personnel for stunts, weapons, fire, vehicles, water, animals, heights, and special effects.

## Release checklist

Confirm final approvals, credits, attribution, captions, audio description where required, subtitle accuracy, loudness, color space, aspect ratios, thumbnail and trailer rights, archive masters, project files, and takedown/contact process. This module is workflow guidance, not legal advice; escalate jurisdiction-specific questions to qualified counsel.
