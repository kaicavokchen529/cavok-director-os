# Color and Image Finishing

## Establish the image contract

Define reference color space, delivery color space, display target, dynamic range, resolution, frame rate, shutter character, grain, sharpness, and legal range before matching shots.

## Normalize before styling

Balance exposure, white balance, contrast, black level, highlight roll-off, saturation, and skin tone before applying a look. Match generated clips by neutral anchors such as skin, wardrobe, neutral surfaces, sky, foliage, and motivated light sources.

## Build the look

Use color to support story geography, time, mood, and power without breaking physical light. Control:

- warm/cool relationship;
- hue separation and color density;
- local contrast and shadow color;
- skin protection;
- highlight behavior;
- atmospheric depth;
- VFX integration and emissive restraint;
- texture, halation, bloom, grain, and lens artifacts.

Do not use a global grade to repair incorrect local lighting or material response.

## Temporal consistency

Check flicker, exposure pumping, color breathing, unstable grain, sharpening halos, denoise trails, motion-vector artifacts, and changing faces or materials. Use temporal tools conservatively; preserve motion detail and avoid waxy skin.

## Multi-source matching

For clips from different models or Unreal, match camera response and texture as well as hue. Align motion blur, depth of field, grain scale, edge sharpness, compression, black floor, and highlight clipping.

## Finishing checklist

```text
[ ] Color management and delivery target are explicit.
[ ] Exposure and white balance match across cuts.
[ ] Skin and key identity colors remain stable.
[ ] VFX light belongs to the environment.
[ ] Blacks and highlights retain intended detail.
[ ] Grain, sharpness, blur, and texture match.
[ ] No temporal flicker, trails, or compression surprises.
[ ] Titles, subtitles, and graphics respect safe areas if present.
```
